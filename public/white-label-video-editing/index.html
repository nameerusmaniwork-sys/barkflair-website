FAQ

Quick answers.

Will my clients know you edited the videos?

No. We never contact your clients, never add our logo or watermark, and deliver files straight to your folder under your brand. You can sign an NDA with us if you need one.

Can you follow a separate style for each of my clients?

Yes. We keep a brand sheet for every client you send us: fonts, colors, caption style, music, intros and outros. Every editor works from the same sheet, so the look stays consistent.

What does white-label video editing cost?

Reels and short-form videos are $35–50 each, social ads $60–80 and real estate tours $75–120. Monthly packages start at $600 for 15 edits. Your first test is free to judge: send one shoot, get 3 edits, and pay only if you would send them to your client.

How fast is turnaround?

Most reels and ads come back in 24–48 hours. Real estate tours and longer edits take 48–72 hours. If you have a rush delivery, tell us the deadline and we plan around it.

What happens if our volume doubles next month?

Move up a package or add edits as you go. We plan capacity a month ahead, so tell us early and your turnaround stays the same.

How do we send footage and get files back?

Share a Google Drive, Dropbox or Frame.io folder. We pick up the footage there, upload drafts for review, and deliver the final files into the same folder, ready for you to send.
