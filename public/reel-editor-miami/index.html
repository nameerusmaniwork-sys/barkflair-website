FAQ

Quick answers.

Are you a reel editor based in Miami?

BarkFlair is a US-registered video editing agency that works remotely with Miami clients. You film or hire a videographer in Miami, upload the footage online, and we deliver finished reels online, usually within 24–48 hours.

Can you add captions in English and Spanish?

Yes. We can caption your reels in English, Spanish, or both on the same video, which works well for Miami’s bilingual audience. Send the script or let us transcribe it.

How much does reel editing cost in Miami?

Your first reel is $30, and you only pay if you love it. After that, reels are $50–150 each depending on length and graphics, and listing tours start at $150. Monthly content packages are quoted on volume.

What kinds of Miami businesses do you edit for?

Real estate agents and luxury condo teams, restaurants and bars, med spas and beauty brands, fitness coaches, car detailers and creators across Miami-Dade and Broward.

Do you film in Miami?

No. We are an editing team. You film on your phone or camera, or send footage from your Miami videographer, and we handle everything after the shoot.

¿Editan reels en español?

Sí. Editamos reels con subtítulos en español, inglés o ambos. Envíe su material por Google Drive y reciba su reel en 24–48 horas. Su primera edición cuesta $30.
