The structure

Four parts. One goal.

Every VSL we edit follows a proven structure, so viewers keep watching until they’re ready to buy.

Part 2

Problem

Part 3

Proof

Demos, results and testimonials, made easy to follow with motion graphics.

FAQ

Quick answers.

How long does a VSL edit take?

Most VSLs are delivered in 3–7 days, depending on length and how much motion graphics they need.

Do you write the script?

You provide the script and footage. While editing, we suggest structure and pacing changes that help hold attention.

Are motion graphics included?

Yes. Kinetic text, simple animated graphics and on-screen proof points are included. Complex custom animation is quoted separately.

Can you cut the VSL into ads?

Yes. We can turn the strongest moments into short ads and social clips, so one shoot fuels your whole funnel.

How many revisions are included?

VSL projects include two rounds of revisions.
