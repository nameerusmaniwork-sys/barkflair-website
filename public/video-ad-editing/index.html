FAQ

Quick answers.

How many hook variations do I get?

Usually three to five opening variations per ad, depending on your package, so you can test what stops the scroll.

Can you edit UGC footage?

Yes. Send the footage from you or your creators and we edit it into UGC-style ads with hooks, captions and a clear CTA.

Which sizes do you deliver?

Every ad comes in the sizes you need: 9:16 for Reels, Stories and TikTok, 4:5 and 1:1 for feeds, and 16:9 for YouTube.

How fast is delivery?

Most ads are delivered within 24–48 hours of receiving your footage.

Do you run the ads too?

Our focus is the creative. If you also need help with social media management, we offer that as a separate service.
