FAQ

Quick answers.

Do you edit for agents in Houston, Dallas, Austin and San Antonio?

Yes. We edit remotely for agents and teams across Texas. You upload your footage online and get the finished videos back in 24–48 hours.

Do you film the property?

No. We are a remote editing team. You, your photographer or your videographer film the listing, and you send us the raw footage through Google Drive or Dropbox.

Can you edit new construction and builder videos?

Yes. We edit model home tours, community and amenity videos, and quick-move-in listing reels for agents and builders.

Can you edit drone footage of land and acreage?

Yes. We edit drone footage of ranches, acreage and Hill Country properties, with property lines, maps and text on screen.

Can I get an unbranded version for the MLS?

Yes. We deliver a branded version for social media and an unbranded version if your MLS requires one.

How much does a listing video cost?

Your first edit is $30. After that, listing reels and tours are $50–150 per video depending on length and footage, and monthly packages are quoted to fit your listings.
