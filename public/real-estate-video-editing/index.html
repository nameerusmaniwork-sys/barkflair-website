FAQ

Quick answers.

What does a real estate video editing service do?

You film the listing, or your photographer does, and send us the raw footage. We turn it into listing reels for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, full property tours, and agent brand videos with captions, music and your branding.

Which states do you work in?

We edit remotely for agents across the United States, with a focus on Florida and Texas. You send footage online and get the finished videos back online, wherever you list.

How fast will I get my listing video?

Most listing reels and tours are delivered within 24–48 hours of receiving the footage, so the video is ready while the listing is new.

Can you edit phone, gimbal and drone footage?

Yes. We edit iPhone and Android footage, gimbal walkthroughs, camera footage and drone shots.

Can I get an unbranded version for the MLS?

Yes. We deliver a branded version for social media and an unbranded version if your MLS requires one.

How much does real estate video editing cost?

Your first edit is $30. After that, listing reels and tours are $50–150 per video depending on length and footage, and monthly agent packages are quoted to fit your listings.
