Hooks & structure

The first two seconds decide everything, so we plan the hook before we make a cut.

  • Pattern-interrupt openings
  • Re-hooks every 3–4 seconds
  • A clear call to action
Captions

Most people watch on mute. Bold, readable captions keep them following along.

  • Animated word-by-word captions
  • Styled to your brand colors
  • Proofread before delivery
Pacing

Pacing & sound

Tight cuts, B-roll and sound design make a talking head feel fast and alive.

  • Jump cuts and zoom punches
  • B-roll and on-screen graphics
  • Music and sound effects

FAQ

Quick answers.

Which platforms do you edit for?

Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and LinkedIn. Videos are delivered in 9:16, with 4:5 or 1:1 versions when you need them.

How many videos can you edit each month?

Anything from a single video to a full month of daily posts. Monthly batches get a lower price per video.

Do you add captions?

Yes. Every video gets animated captions styled to your brand, and we proofread them before delivery.

How fast is delivery?

Most short-form videos are delivered within 24–48 hours of receiving your footage.

How many revisions are included?

Regular projects include two rounds of revisions. The $30 first edit includes one.
