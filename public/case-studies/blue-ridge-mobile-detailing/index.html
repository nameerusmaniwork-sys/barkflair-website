Blue Ridge Mobile Detailing: from $4K to $54K in monthly sales

Between January and July 2026, while running BarkFlair video ads and website, Blue Ridge grew from $4K to $54K in monthly sales. Here is the work behind it, including one $23 ad test that brought in 16 leads and 4 sales.

Blue Ridge Mobile Detailing is a mobile detailing business. We worked with them on their marketing from start to finish: scripted Meta ads, social media video editing, a TV commercial from shot list to voiceover, and their website and SEO.

The bigger picture: $4K to $54K a month

In January 2026, Blue Ridge was doing about $4,000 in gross monthly sales. By July 2026 that number was $54,000 a month, roughly 13 times higher. During that time the business was running our scripted Meta ads, social video and new website.

Monthly sales figures shared by the owner of Blue Ridge Mobile Detailing. They cover the whole business, which also grew through the owner’s own work and other factors, so they are not a guarantee of results.

The ad test

Use short video ads to sell a specific, higher-ticket service: the Woolwax Winter Protection package at $399, and see whether paid social could bring in real bookings on a small budget.

What we made

A Meta video ad built around a single offer, the $399 Woolwax Winter Protection package, with captions, on-screen text and one clear call to action, sized for Reels, Stories and Feed.

What we took from it

  • One offer, one price. The ad sold a single package instead of listing every service.
  • Seasonal offers work. A service tied to the time of year gives people a reason to book now.
  • Test small first. A small budget was enough to prove the creative before spending more.
