FAQ

Quick answers.

Are you a video editor based in Florida?

BarkFlair is a US-registered video editing agency that works remotely. We work with businesses, agents and creators across Florida. You upload your footage online, and we deliver finished videos online, usually within 24–48 hours.

What kinds of videos do you edit for Florida businesses?

Instagram Reels and TikToks, Meta and TikTok ads, real estate listing reels and tours, car detailing ads, YouTube talking-head videos and Shorts, podcasts, VSLs, UGC ads, explainers and commercials.

Which Florida cities do you work with?

Clients anywhere in Florida, including Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville, Fort Lauderdale, St. Petersburg, Naples and Sarasota.

How much does video editing cost?

Your first edit is $30, and you only pay if you love it. After that, short-form videos are $50–150, YouTube videos $150–400, car detailing ad test packs $250 and VSLs from $400.

Do you film the videos?

No. We are an editing team. You film on your phone or camera, or send footage from your videographer, and we handle everything after that.

How do we work together?

Send your footage by Google Drive or Dropbox, we plan the hook and structure, you get the first cut fast, and we revise until you are happy. Two rounds of revisions are included.
