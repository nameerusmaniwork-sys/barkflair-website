10 Instagram reel ideas for Miami realtors

Ten Instagram reel ideas that work for Miami real estate agents: listing reels, neighborhood guides, guess-the-price and agent brand videos.

Miami buyers scroll through more luxury listings than almost anywhere in the country. To stand out, your reels need ideas, not just footage. Here are ten that work for Miami agents.

Listing reels

  1. The view first. Open on the balcony or the water, then walk back inside. In Miami, the view is often the product.
  2. Guess the price. Show the home, ask viewers to guess in the comments, reveal the price at the end. Comments push reach.
  3. Morning to night. The same room at sunrise and at golden hour. It sells the lifestyle, not just the square footage.
  4. The one feature. A whole reel about the rooftop pool, the wine room or the private dock.

Agent brand reels

  1. Neighborhood in 20 seconds. Brickell vs. Coconut Grove vs. Miami Beach: who each one is for.
  2. What $X gets you. The same budget in three different Miami neighborhoods.
  3. Myth vs. fact. Flood zones, HOA fees, insurance, and condo inspections, explained simply.
  4. Just sold story. The client, the challenge and the result, told in three cuts.

Engagement reels

  1. POV: your Saturday in this home. Coffee on the terrace, pool, sunset. Buyers picture themselves there.
  2. Behind the scenes. Staging day, photo day or a showing. It builds trust in you, not just the listing.

Edit them for the feed

Whatever the idea, the edit decides whether people watch: a hook in the first second, fast cuts, captions on every word, and text kept inside Instagram's safe zone so the app doesn't cover it.
