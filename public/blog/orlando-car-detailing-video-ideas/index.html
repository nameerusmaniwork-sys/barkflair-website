7 video ideas for Orlando car detailers that bring in bookings

Seven video ideas for Orlando and Central Florida car detailers: before-and-afters, love bug season, ceramic coating water tests and more, with filming tips.

Orlando detailers have a big advantage: the work is visual, satisfying and easy to film on a phone. Here are video ideas that turn that work into bookings, plus how to film each one.

1. The 3-second before and after

Open on the dirtiest shot you have, cut straight to the finished result, then show the process. Film the before in good light and the after from the same angle so the change is obvious.

2. "We come to you" in Orlando

Show your setup arriving at a driveway or office parking lot. In Orlando traffic, not having to drive anywhere is a selling point on its own.

3. Love bug season clean-up

Close-ups of a bug-covered front bumper, then the clean result. Every Central Florida driver knows this pain, which makes it a perfect seasonal hook.

4. Ceramic coating water test

Pour water over a coated panel and film the beading in slow motion. Then explain why coating matters in the Florida sun.

5. Interior deep clean

Steam, extraction and the stained seat coming clean. Family cars, rideshare drivers and rental fleets all relate to this.

6. Package explainer

Show what each package includes and its price. It answers questions before people message you, so the leads you get are ready to book.

7. Day in the life

The early start, three jobs, the final reveal. People book detailers they feel they know.
