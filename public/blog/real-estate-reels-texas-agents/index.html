Real estate reels for Texas agents: what to film and how to edit

A guide for Texas agents in Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, Austin and San Antonio: what to film for listing reels and how to edit them.

Texas buyers scroll through listings from Houston to the Hill Country every day. Reels that get watched lead with the best part of the home and keep moving. Here is what to film for every Texas listing and how to edit it.

Lead with what Texas buyers want

  • Outdoor living. Covered patios, outdoor kitchens, pools and big backyards are often the hero shot.
  • Space. Open floor plans, game rooms, home offices and three-car garages. Show the size with wide, steady shots.
  • New construction and communities. Amenity centers, trails and pools sell the lifestyle, especially in the suburbs around Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston and Austin.
  • Land and acreage. For rural and Hill Country listings, a drone shot of the property line and views does the work.

What to film for every listing

  1. The hero shot: the single feature a buyer will remember.
  2. One smooth walkthrough from the front door to the backyard.
  3. Three to five detail shots of finishes and upgrades.
  4. The neighborhood: the community pool, the school nearby or the drive into downtown.
  5. A 10-second intro from you with your name, the neighborhood and why the home stands out.

How to edit it

  • Open on the hero shot with the price, the city and two key features on screen.
  • Cut every one to two seconds through the walkthrough, on the beat.
  • Add captions to anything you say, because most people watch on mute.
  • Keep text inside Instagram's safe zone so the app doesn't cover it.
  • End with one clear next step, like "DM me for a private showing".
