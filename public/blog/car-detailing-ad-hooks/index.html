Car detailing ads: 3 hooks that book more jobs

Three proven hook angles for car detailing video ads, how to test them on Meta, and exactly what to film for your next detailing ad.

Detailing is one of the most visual services there is. Foam, pressure washing, paint correction and that final wipe are made for short video. But most detailing ads still open on a logo or a slow pan. The first three seconds decide whether someone watches, so here are three hook angles we use for detailing businesses, and how to film for them.

Hook 1: the contrast

"The same detail we give exotic cars." If you work on high-end vehicles, open on that footage, then show that everyday drivers get the same standard of care. It sets a premium bar and makes your regular packages feel like a great deal.

Hook 2: the local call-out

"[Your city], your car could look like this by tonight." Naming the city tells local viewers the ad is for them, and "by tonight" sells speed and convenience, especially for mobile detailers who come to the customer.

Hook 3: the transformation

"This is what $200 gets you." Open on the dirtiest before shot, then cut to the finished result with the price on screen. A clear price filters out the wrong customers and makes the offer concrete.

How to test the hooks

Keep the body and the offer the same in every version and change only the first three seconds. Run the versions side by side with the same budget, and the one with the lowest cost per lead tells you which angle your customers respond to. Then make more ads around the winner.

What to film for detailing ads

  • A dirty before shot in good light: muddy wheels, pet hair, stained seats or swirl marks.
  • Satisfying process clips of 3–5 seconds each: foam cannon, pressure rinse, steam, extraction and polishing.
  • The after reveal: a slow walk around the finished car, water beading, and a close-up of the shine.
  • You or your team on the job, so customers see who will be working on their car.
  • Clean audio of the process. The sounds of foam and pressure washing are part of what makes detailing videos addictive.
