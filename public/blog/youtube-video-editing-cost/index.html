How much does YouTube video editing cost?

What drives the price of editing a talking-head YouTube video, what BarkFlair charges per video and per Short, and how to keep editing costs down.

YouTube editing prices vary a lot, from cheap freelancers to full production teams. Here's what actually drives the price of editing a talking-head video, and exactly what we charge.

What affects the price

  • Length of the finished video. A 6-minute video takes far less time than a 25-minute deep dive.
  • How much raw footage there is. Two hours of recording to cut down is more work than a tight 15-minute take.
  • B-roll and graphics. Simple cuts and captions cost less than custom motion graphics, maps or animated diagrams.
  • Turnaround. Rush delivery costs more than a regular weekly schedule.
  • Volume. A steady number of videos every month gets a lower price per video than one-off edits.

What's included in every edit

  • Dead air, mistakes and retakes removed
  • Zooms and reframes to keep the shot moving
  • B-roll, screen recordings and motion text
  • Captions, music, sound effects and audio cleanup
  • Two rounds of revisions

How to keep editing costs down

  1. Outline the video before you film, so there is less footage to cut.
  2. Record clean audio with a simple lav or USB mic.
  3. Send B-roll and links with your footage instead of asking us to find them.
  4. Book a monthly package for a lower price per video.
