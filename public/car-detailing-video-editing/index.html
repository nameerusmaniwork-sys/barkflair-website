How we test

Three hooks. One winner.

The first three seconds decide whether someone watches. So every ad comes with three openings to test.

Hook A

The contrast

“The same detail we give exotic cars.” Premium footage sets the standard, then the offer feels like a steal.

Hook B

The local call-out

“Your city, your car could look like this by tonight.” Speaks straight to local drivers.

Hook C

The transformation

“This is what $X gets you.” The dirtiest before shot, the cleanest after, and the price on screen.

Hook D

The test

Same body and offer in every version, so the data shows which hook books the most jobs.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Do you film the cars?

No. You film on your phone while you work, and send us the clips through Google Drive or Dropbox. We tell you exactly what to film with a simple shot list.

What do I get in the $250 ad test pack?

One main Meta ad with three different hooks, each delivered in 9:16 for Reels and Stories and 4:5 for Feed (six files), plus ad copy for each hook and two rounds of revisions.

Do you run the ads for me?

We focus on the creative. You or your ads person run the ads in Meta Ads Manager, and we deliver every file named and sized for the placements you use. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Once the invoice is paid, you own the final videos and can use them on any channel, including paid ads.

How fast is delivery?

Reels are usually delivered in 24–48 hours. A full ad test pack takes 4–5 days, including a creative brief you approve before editing starts.

Do you work with mobile detailers outside Florida?

Yes. We work remotely with detailing businesses across the United States: mobile detailers, ceramic coating and PPF shops, car washes and auto spas.
