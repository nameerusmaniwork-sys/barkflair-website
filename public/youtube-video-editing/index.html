FAQ

Quick answers.

What kind of YouTube videos do you edit?

Talking-head videos: education, commentary, business, finance, coaching, tech and personal brand channels, plus podcasts and interviews filmed for YouTube.

How do I send my footage?

Upload your raw recording, plus any B-roll, screen recordings and links, to Google Drive or Dropbox. If you have a script or outline, send that too.

How long does a YouTube edit take?

Most 8–20 minute talking-head videos are delivered in 2–4 days. With a monthly package we work to a regular weekly schedule.

Do you make Shorts from my videos?

Yes. We cut the strongest moments from each long video into vertical Shorts with captions, ready for YouTube Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

Do you make thumbnails?

Our focus is the edit. We can export clean, high-quality frames from your video to use in your thumbnails.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions on every video. The $30 first edit includes one.
