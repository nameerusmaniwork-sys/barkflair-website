FAQ

Quick answers.

Do you film the property?

No. We are a remote editing team. You, your photographer or your videographer film the listing, and you send us the raw footage through Google Drive or Dropbox.

Can you edit drone and gimbal footage?

Yes. We edit phone, gimbal, camera and drone footage, including aerial shots of waterfront homes and neighborhoods.

Can I get an unbranded version for the MLS?

Yes. We can deliver a branded version for social media and an unbranded version if your MLS requires one.

How fast will I get my listing video?

Most listing reels and tours are delivered within 24–48 hours, so your video is ready while the listing is still new.

Do you edit for Airbnb and vacation rentals?

Yes. We edit property tours and promo reels for short-term rental hosts and property managers across Florida.

How much does a listing video cost?

Your first edit is $30. After that, listing reels and tours are $50–150 per video depending on length and footage, and monthly packages are quoted to fit your listings.
