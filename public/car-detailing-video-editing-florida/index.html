FAQ

Quick answers.

Do you make car detailing ads for Florida businesses?

Yes. We work remotely with detailing businesses across Florida, including Miami, Orlando, Tampa and Jacksonville. You film on your phone, upload the clips, and we deliver finished Meta ads and reels.

Are you based in Florida?

No. BarkFlair is a US-registered video editing agency that works remotely. We focus on Florida detailers, so the ads speak to Florida drivers, weather and cities.

What does a Florida detailing ad focus on?

Usually the problem Florida drivers already have, like sun fading, salt air, love bugs, rain spots and sand, then your fix and your price. We test three hooks so the data shows which angle books the most jobs.

What is in the $250 ad test pack?

One main Meta ad with three different hooks, each in 9:16 for Reels and Stories and 4:5 for Feed (six files), ad copy for each hook and two rounds of revisions.

Do you run the ads?

We create the ads. You or your ads person run them in Meta Ads Manager, and you pay ad spend directly to Meta.

How fast is delivery?

Reels in 24–48 hours. A full ad test pack in 4–5 days, including a brief with the hooks that you approve before editing starts.
