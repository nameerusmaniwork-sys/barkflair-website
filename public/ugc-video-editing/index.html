FAQ

Quick answers.

Do you provide the creators?

No. We edit the footage you or your creators supply. Send us their raw clips and we turn them into ready-to-run ads.

Is phone footage okay?

Yes. UGC is meant to look native, so phone footage works well. We clean up audio, color and framing so it still looks professional.

How many hook variations do I get?

Usually three to five opening variations per ad, so you can test what stops the scroll.

Can you add product shots and screen recordings?

Yes. We mix creator clips with product B-roll, close-ups, screen recordings and on-screen benefits.

How fast is delivery?

Most UGC ads are delivered within 24–48 hours of receiving your footage.
