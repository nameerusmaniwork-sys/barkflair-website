FAQ

Quick answers.

Which lengths do you edit?

Standard 15, 30 and 60-second spots, plus vertical and square cut-downs for social media and YouTube.

Can you deliver files ready for TV or streaming?

Yes. Send us the delivery specs from your TV station, streaming platform or ad network, and we export to them, including the required file format and audio levels.

Can you add legal disclaimers and supers?

Yes. We add on-screen disclaimers, phone numbers, offers and lower thirds, which is common for law firm, finance and automotive commercials.

Do you film the commercial?

No. We are an editing team. You, your production company or your videographer film it, and we turn the footage into a finished commercial.

How long does a commercial edit take?

Usually 2–5 days, depending on length, footage and motion graphics. We confirm the timeline before we start.

How is pricing worked out?

Commercials are quoted per project, based on length, number of versions and motion graphics. Social cut-downs start at $50 per ad.
