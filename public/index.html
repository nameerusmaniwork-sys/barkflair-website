Anatomy of a BarkFlair edit

Every second has a job.

Here's how we map a 30-second ad before we start cutting. Each beat is built to stop the viewer from swiping away.

00:0000:0500:1000:1500:2000:2500:30
0–3s

Hook

A bold line or visual that earns the next 3 seconds.

3–10s

Problem

Name the pain your viewer already feels, fast.

10–22s

Proof & solution

Show the product working, with re-hooks every 3–4 seconds to hold retention.

22–30s

CTA

One clear action with urgency and a reason to click now.

FAQ

Quick answers.

How fast will I get my video?

Most short-form videos and ads are delivered within 24–48 hours. VSLs usually take 3–7 days depending on length and motion graphics.

How many revisions do I get?

The $30 trial edit includes one revision. Regular projects include two rounds, so you get exactly what you want.

What do I need to send?

Your raw footage, your logo and brand colors if you have them, and a few example videos you like. We handle everything else.

Who will I be working with?

BarkFlair is a US registered company with a global production team. That means competitive pricing and edits that are often ready by your next morning.

How do payments work?

You pay per video or per bundle, upfront for the trial and by milestone for larger projects. We invoice in USD.
